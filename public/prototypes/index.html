Design Prototypes

5 directions for MedGemma's UI. Click to explore.

01

Deep Space

Luminous cards floating on pitch black. Zero chrome, keyboard-first. Cmd+K palette for navigation.

 02

The Artifact

Response as full-screen document. Thread strip on left. Hover reference cards. The chat is secondary.

 03

Monochrome Brutalist

Pure black and white. Massive typography. No rounded corners. Clinical values are the only color.

 04

Thread Canvas

Horizontal card canvas. Branch conversations, pin and compare responses side-by-side. Minimap navigation.

 05

Ambient Glass

Living gradient mesh background. Frosted glass cards with depth layers. Translucent sidebar overlay.
