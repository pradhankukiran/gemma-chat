Differential Diagnosis & Immediate Workup

Primary Differential

Acute Coronary Syndrome (STEMI/NSTEMI) — highest probability given substernal pain with left arm radiation, diaphoresis, and multiple cardiovascular risk factors. Current vitals: BP 158/94, HR 102.

Also consider: aortic dissection (bilateral BP, CT-A), PE (Wells score), tension pneumothorax.

Immediate Orders

STAT 12-lead ECG. Troponin serial q3h. CBC, BMP, coags. Aspirin 325mg chewed now. Telemetry, two large-bore IVs, type and screen.

ECG shows ST elevation in II, III, aVF. Troponin I is 2.4 ng/mL. What's the protocol?

Inferior STEMI — Acute Protocol

Immediate Action

Activate cardiac catheterization lab. Door-to-balloon target: <90 minutes. Troponin I 2.4 ng/mL (ref <0.04) confirms significant myocardial injury.

Pharmacotherapy

Ticagrelor 180mg PO loading. Heparin 60 U/kg IV bolus (max 4,000 U). Continue aspirin. Consider Nitroglycerin 0.4mg SL unless RV involvement suspected.

Critical — RV Involvement

Inferior STEMI carries ~40% risk of RV involvement. Obtain right-sided V4R lead. If positive: no nitrates, no diuretics. Volume resuscitate with NS 250mL boluses.
