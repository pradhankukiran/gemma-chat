Inferior STEMI:
Acute Management Protocol

ST elevation confirmed in leads II, III, aVF consistent with inferior myocardial infarction. Troponin I at 2.4 ng/mL (ref <0.04) confirms significant myocardial necrosis. Immediate PCI is the definitive treatment.

01 Immediate Actions (0–10 minutes)
Activate cardiac catheterization lab immediately. Door-to-balloon target: <90 minutes. Contact interventional cardiology on-call.

Confirm Aspirin

Aspirin (Acetylsalicylic Acid)

Irreversible COX inhibitor. In ACS: 162–325mg chewed for rapid buccal absorption. Reduces mortality by 23% per ISIS-2 trial.

325mg chewed has been administered. If not, give immediately. Administer Ticagrelor 180mg loading dose orally. If ticagrelor is unavailable or contraindicated, substitute Clopidogrel 600mg loading dose.

Initiate anticoagulation with Heparin

Unfractionated Heparin (UFH)

Standard dosing for PCI: 60 U/kg bolus (max 4000 U), then 12 U/kg/hr (max 1000 U/hr). Target aPTT 50–70 seconds. Adjust for renal function.

60 U/kg IV bolus (max 4,000 U) prior to transfer to cath lab.

02 Hemodynamic Monitoring
Parameter Current Target Intervention if abnormal
Blood Pressure 158/94 mmHg SBP 100–140 IV NTG titrated, avoid if RV involvement
Heart Rate 102 bpm 60–100 bpm Beta-blocker if no contraindication
SpO2 — ≥94% Supplemental O2 if below target
RV function Assess — Right-sided leads, avoid nitrates/diuretics

Critical consideration for inferior STEMI: Obtain right-sided ECG (V4R) to assess for RV involvement. If present, avoid nitrates and aggressive diuresis. Provide volume resuscitation with NS 250mL boluses for hypotension.

03 Adjunctive Medications

Morphine 2–4mg IV for pain unresponsive to nitrates (use cautiously — may reduce preload). Metoprolol 5mg IV if HR remains >100 and SBP >120, no signs of heart failure, and no RV involvement. Hold ACE inhibitor initiation until hemodynamically stable post-PCI.

Atorvastatin 80mg high-intensity statin — can initiate in ED regardless of baseline lipids.
